## Why placeholders are visibly labeled

The specification forbids fictional robot photos or simulation results. The included SVGs are intentionally abstract diagrams with a visible `TECHNICAL PLACEHOLDER` label. They are layout assets only and should be replaced by project evidence, not presented as results.
